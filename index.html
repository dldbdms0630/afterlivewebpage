About the work

Every day we leave behind traces of our identity in the digital realm.

after.live is an immersive installation that explores the digital afterlife: how images and data of us persist long after we're gone, and are continuously remixed, distorted, and reanimated by AI systems. What does identity become when only our digital representations remain?

What we currently consider vital sources of information may evolve into digital charnel houses — vast repositories of data belonging almost entirely to the deceased. after.live is a glimpse into what your data might be up to after you've left this world.

You will lie beneath a projection of yourself. The images that you submit as representations of yourself will be processed and distorted by a machine, dissolving slowly above you. Your silhouette will hold them briefly before letting go.
